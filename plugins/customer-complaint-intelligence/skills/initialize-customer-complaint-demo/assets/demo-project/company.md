# Northstar Linen Services

Northstar rents and delivers sheets, towels and other linen to hotels,
restaurants and spas. Customers contact a shared support inbox when a delivery
is late, incomplete, damaged, stained, billed incorrectly or needs to change.

The team has a large volume of messages but no structured view of what is
happening across customers. The demo starts with that ordinary mess and lets a
human use Codex to learn what deserves attention.
